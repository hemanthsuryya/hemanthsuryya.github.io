What We Make

Our Product Range

Engineered for demanding automotive applications — from suspension systems to engine components.

01

Compression Springs

High-load helical compression springs designed for automotive suspension, engine valve systems, and chassis components.

  • Wire diameter: 0.5 – 25mm
  • Free length up to 500mm
  • Carbon, alloy & stainless steel
  • Shot peened & preset options
02

Extension Springs

Precision-wound tension springs with various hook configurations for braking systems, throttle mechanisms, and body panels.

  • Initial tension control
  • Multiple end configurations
  • Fatigue life tested
  • Custom coatings available
03

Torsion Springs

Angular deflection springs for door hinges, parking mechanisms, seat recliner systems, and transmission assemblies.

  • Right & left-hand wound
  • Single & double torsion
  • Tanged & straight arms
  • Heat treated for durability
04

Die Springs

Heavy-duty rectangular wire springs built for stamping dies, press tools, and fixture applications in automotive manufacturing lines.

  • ISO 10243 compliant
  • 4 load ratings available
  • Colour-coded by load
  • Up to 1 million cycles
05

Precision Tooling

Custom jigs, fixtures, and press tools manufactured to drawing for automotive OEM production and sub-assembly processes.

  • CNC machined components
  • Hardened & ground tooling
  • Tool steel & carbide inserts
  • CMM inspection reports
06

Custom Solutions

Bespoke engineering for specialized requirements — from wire forms and clips to multi-component spring assemblies.

  • Design support available
  • Prototype to production
  • Low MOQ for new projects
  • PPAP documentation
